## Who it is for

OpenDocBot has the same features on the free and commercial tiers. The commercial license is about usage rights, support and commercial terms, not about unlocking a different product. OpenDocBot for teams is intended for:

- Organizations with more than 30 people using OpenDocBot. If your team stays at 30 or fewer, you can use the free tier, including all features (self-hosting, SSO and managed configuration).
- Organizations that need a commercial agreement, invoice, purchase order or vendor onboarding.
- Teams that want a named support contact and help with deployment.
- Partners and service providers that deploy OpenDocBot for clients or want to whitelabel it.

## What the commercial license covers

The product does not change. A commercial license covers the right to use it beyond the free tier and, depending on the plan, adds commercial support and services.

- **More than 30 users:** the free tier covers up to 30 users. The commercial license covers the rest.
- **Commercial terms:** an annual agreement, invoice, purchase order and vendor onboarding.
- **Support:** Team includes email support. Business adds priority email support and an onboarding call. Enterprise adds deployment support and custom terms.
- **Whitelabel:** Enterprise includes the right to replace OpenDocBot branding with your own.

::: tip The product stays the same
Self-hosting, SSO, managed configuration, BYOK, local models, human-in-the-loop, skills and MCP are the same on every tier. They do not require a commercial license. See the [license](/docs/license) for the exact terms.
:::

## FAQ

::: details Do you process our documents?
No. The hosted add-in runs in the user's Office client. Document content, selections and prompts go directly from the user's device to the AI provider they configured. OpenDocBot does not operate a proxy for the hosted add-in. If you self-host, you operate the server and any optional proxy yourself.
:::

::: details Do we need our own AI provider?
Yes. OpenDocBot is BYOK. You connect your own API key, cloud endpoint, gateway or local model, and you pay the provider directly. This is also why we do not mark up tokens.
:::

::: details Can we self-host?
Yes. OpenDocBot can be self-hosted anytime. Self-hosting unlocks [SSO](/docs/enterprise/sso) and [managed configuration](/docs/enterprise/managed-configuration). See the [self-hosting guide](/docs/selfhosting) for further guidance.
:::

::: details Can we use Microsoft Entra ID?
Yes. SSO uses OIDC. See the [SSO guide](/docs/enterprise/sso) for the general setup, and the [Microsoft Entra ID guide](/docs/enterprise/entra-id) if you use Entra.
:::

::: details Does it work with Microsoft 365?
Yes. OpenDocBot is a Microsoft 365 add-in for Word, Excel and PowerPoint on desktop and web. It is independent from Microsoft and does not require Copilot.
:::

::: details What about support?
Email support is best-effort, with an average response time of 2 business days. There is no formal SLA. Enterprise agreements can include deployment support.
:::

::: details Can we whitelabel it?
Yes. Whitelabeling is part of the Enterprise plan. It includes the right to replace the OpenDocBot branding with your own name and logo.
:::

::: details Do you offer a DPA?
We can discuss data processing terms during the intro. Because the hosted add-in does not receive document content, prompts or API keys, the scope is limited. We do not publish a DPA template at this stage.
:::

::: details We need audit logs, RBAC or an admin console. Can you help?
Those features are not available today. We are working with early enterprise customers on administration and auditability. If they are blockers for you, tell us in the intro so we can scope them with you.
:::

::: details Is OpenDocBot open source?
OpenDocBot is fair-code and source-available. The code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/opendocbot/opendocbot), and the [license](/docs/license) explains what is free and what requires a commercial license.
:::
